# Design Matrix: The Combinatorial DNA

This matrix breaks down design into atomic elements. By combining these dimensions, we can generate over **10 million** unique visual variations (60 Styles × 15 Layouts × 20 Palettes × 30 Materials × 20 Lighting setups).

## 1. Core Styles (The Soul)
*Refer to `references/styles.md` for the full list. Below are category attributes for logic:*
- **Tech & Future** [Digital]: High gloss, neon, precise.
- **Art & Emotion** [Analog]: Organic, rough, fluid.
- **Cute & Lively** [Digital/Analog Mix]: Soft, rounded, bright.
- **Industrial & Texture** [Physical]: Heavy, tactile, rough.
- **Retro & Nostalgic** [Analog]: Aged, grain, faded.
- **Nature Elements** [Organic]: Living, growing, chaotic.

## 2. Composition Patterns (The Grid/Layout)
*How the "格子" (grids) are drawn.*
- **WeChat Dual-Panel (Standard)**: Strict split-screen layout. Left 70% is a landscape main cover (Subject + Title), Right 30% is a square icon/logo for sharing.
- **WeChat Dual-Panel (Seamless)**: A single wide panoramic image where the left 70% works as the main cover and the right 30% works as a standalone icon.
- **WeChat Dual-Panel (Contrast)**: Left side complex/detailed, Right side minimal/bold text (for small icon visibility).
- **Central Focus**: Subject/Text dead center, symmetrical balance. Powerful and stable.
- **Rule of Thirds**: Key elements placed at 1/3 intersection points. Dynamic and professional.
- **Floating Gravity**: Elements floating weightlessly, no distinct ground line. Dreamy and modern.
- **Split Screen**: 50/50 or 30/70 vertical/horizontal split. High contrast duality.
- **Grid System**: Strict Swiss-style grid alignment. Organized and information-dense.
- **Diagonal Tension**: Elements arranged on a diagonal axis. High energy and movement.
- **Frame within Frame**: Central content enclosed in a stylistic border/window. Depth-inducing.
- **Typographic Mask**: Image inside the heavy text strokes. Bold and integrated.
- **Negative Space Dominance**: Small subject, 80% empty space. Minimalist and breathable.
- **Chaotic Collage**: Overlapping layers, mixed media feel. Rebellious and artistic.
- **Isometric View**: 30-degree angle 3D perspective. Technical and structured.
- **Isometric Code (HTML/CSS)**: Pure CSS implementation of isometric shapes (Cubes/Bubbles). Interactive and clean.
- **Macro Zoom**: Extreme close-up on texture/details, text overlaid. Intimate and tactile.
- **Circular Composition**: Elements arranged in a circle or mandala pattern. Harmonious.
- **Z-Pattern**: Visual flow from top-left to bottom-right. Natural reading path.
- **Perspective Tunnel**: Elements leading into a vanishing point. Immersive depth.

## 3. Color Palettes (The Mood)
- **Cyber Neon**: Hot Pink (#FF00FF) + Cyan (#00FFFF) + Deep Black.
- **Morandi Calm**: Sage Green + Dusty Rose + Slate Blue (Low saturation).
- **Industrial Hazard**: Safety Yellow + Black + Concrete Grey.
- **Royal Luxury**: Deep Gold + Midnight Blue + Velvet Red.
- **Fresh Vitamin**: Orange + Lime Green + White.
- **Ink & Snow**: Pure Black + White + Subtle Red Stamp.
- **Vaporwave**: Pastel Pink + Baby Blue + Mint.
- **Earth Tones**: Terracotta + Olive + Sand.
- **Deep Ocean**: Navy + Teal + Bioluminescent Blue.
- **Monochrome + Pop**: Grayscale image with one single bright accent color (e.g., Red).
- **Sunset Grade**: Purple fading to Orange and Yellow.
- **Acid House**: Clashing high-saturation Green and Purple.
- **Porcelain**: White + Blue patterns + Gold cracks.
- **Matte Pastel**: Chalky textural colors, non-reflective.
- **High Contrast B&W**: Stark noir style, no mid-tones.

## 4. Material & Textures (The Touch)
- **Frosted Glass (Glassmorphism)**: Blurring background, white outline, semi-transparent.
- **Liquid Metal**: Chrome, reflective, fluid shapes.
- **Washi Paper**: Fibrous, organic, soft uneven surface.
- **Holographic Foil**: Rainbow reflections, metallic sheen.
- **Rough Concrete**: Industrial, porous, brutalist.
- **Silk/Satin**: Soft folds, smooth highlights, luxury feel.
- **Glitch/Pixel**: Digital noise, screen tearing, artifacts.
- **Acrylic/Plastic**: Glossy, synthetic, vibrant, semi-transparent.
- **Kraft Paper**: Cardboard texture, recycled feel, warm.
- **Neon Gas**: Glowing tubes, glass housing, light emission.
- **Vantablack**: Light-absorbing matte black, void-like.
- **Crystal/Diamond**: Refractive, sharp facets, prismatic light.
- **Velvet/Felt**: Soft, light-absorbing, cozy.
- **Rust/Patina**: Aged metal, oxidation, history.
- **Biological/Organic**: Cell structures, leaf veins, skin texture.

## 5. Lighting & Atmosphere (The Vibe)
- **Cinematic Rim Light**: Backlight highlighting edges/silhouettes.
- **Volumetric God Rays**: Beams of light cutting through fog/dust.
- **Studio Softbox**: Even, shadowless, commercial product look.
- **Cyberpunk Street**: Multi-colored light sources (neon signs) in rain.
- **Natural Window Light**: Soft, directional, casting window frame shadows.
- **Bioluminescence**: Light coming from within the objects/plants.
- **Hard Flash**: Harsh shadows, high contrast, paparazzi style.
- **Golden Hour**: Warm, low-angle sunlight, long shadows.
- **Blue Hour**: Cold, twilight, deep blue ambient light.
- **Projection Mapping**: Patterns projected onto surfaces.
- **Double Exposure**: Silhouettes filled with another image/light.
- **Tyndall Effect**: Light scattering through particles/smoke.
- **Bokeh Field**: Out-of-focus background lights.
- **Rembrandt Lighting**: Triangle of light on the subject, dramatic.
- **Flat Lighting**: No shadows, illustration/2D look.

## 6. Typography DNA (The Voice)
- **Kinetic Type**: Moving, blurred, or stretched text suggesting motion.
- **Brutalist Bold**: Massive, blocky sans-serif, taking up space.
- **Elegant Serif**: Thin strokes, high contrast, fashion editorial look.
- **Handwritten Script**: Personal, messy, or elegant penmanship.
- **Pixel/8-bit**: Retro digital aesthetic.
- **Stencil/Military**: Utilitarian, broken strokes.
- **3D Inflated**: Bubble-like, soft, tactile 3D text.
- **Distorted/Warped**: Melted, liquid, or wave-distorted text.
- **Minimalist Thin**: Hairline weight, clean and modern.
- **Calligraphic Brush**: Ink texture, variable width, traditional.
