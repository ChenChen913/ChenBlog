# Tailwind CSS Style Mapping

This reference maps the `Core Styles` from `design-matrix.md` to concrete Tailwind CSS classes for the `layout_template.md`.

## 1. Tech & Future (科技未来)
*   **Background**: `bg-slate-900`, `bg-[url('https://source.unsplash.com/random/1600x900/?cyberpunk')]`, `bg-gradient-to-br from-gray-900 via-blue-900 to-black`
*   **Text Color**: `text-cyan-400`, `text-transparent bg-clip-text bg-gradient-to-r from-cyan-400 to-blue-600`
*   **Font**: `font-mono`, `tracking-widest`
*   **Decoration**: `border-2 border-cyan-500`, `shadow-[0_0_20px_rgba(34,211,238,0.5)]`

## 2. Art & Emotion (艺术情感)
*   **Background**: `bg-stone-100`, `bg-[#fdfbf7]` (Warm Paper)
*   **Text Color**: `text-stone-800`, `text-gray-600`
*   **Font**: `font-serif`, `italic`, `tracking-wide`
*   **Decoration**: `border-l-4 border-red-800` (Seal style), `opacity-80`

## 3. Cute & Lively (活泼可爱)
*   **Background**: `bg-yellow-100`, `bg-gradient-to-tr from-pink-200 to-yellow-200`
*   **Text Color**: `text-pink-600`, `text-purple-600`
*   **Font**: `font-sans`, `rounded-full` (if available), `tracking-tight`
*   **Decoration**: `rounded-3xl`, `border-4 border-white`, `shadow-lg`

## 4. Industrial & Texture (工业质感)
*   **Background**: `bg-zinc-800`, `bg-[url('texture.png')]`
*   **Text Color**: `text-yellow-500` (Safety Yellow), `text-white`
*   **Font**: `font-black`, `uppercase`
*   **Decoration**: `border-dashed border-2 border-yellow-500`

## 5. Retro & Nostalgic (复古怀旧)
*   **Background**: `bg-orange-50`, `sepia`
*   **Text Color**: `text-red-900`, `text-amber-900`
*   **Font**: `font-serif`, `uppercase`
*   **Decoration**: `border-double border-4 border-amber-900`

## 6. Nature Elements (自然元素)
*   **Background**: `bg-emerald-50`, `bg-gradient-to-b from-green-50 to-emerald-100`
*   **Text Color**: `text-emerald-900`
*   **Font**: `font-sans`, `font-light`
*   **Decoration**: `rounded-tr-[50px] rounded-bl-[50px]` (Leaf shape)
