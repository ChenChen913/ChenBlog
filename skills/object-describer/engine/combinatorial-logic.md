# 🎲 The Engine: Combinatorial Logic (365 Days Unique)

## The Formula
To ensure no two images are alike, we use a randomized combinatorial approach.

**`Prompt = [Base Layout] + [Material A] + [Material B (Accent)] + [Lighting] + [Color Palette] + [Micro-Innovation]`**

## 1. The Factor Pools (The DNA)

### A. Base Materials (Background/Container - 60% Surface)
*Select 1 based on `(Day of Year * Hour) % 10`. CRITICAL: This material applies to the BACKGROUND/PODIUM/UI, NOT the subject itself (unless subject is abstract).*
1.  **Frosted Acrylic**: Translucent, soft blur (Apple style).
2.  **Raw Concrete**: Industrial, porous, grey.
3.  **Polished Marble**: Carrara white with grey veins.
4.  **Brushed Aluminum**: Tech, sleek, anisotropic reflections.
5.  **Washi Paper**: Fibrous, organic, soft light transmission.
6.  **Liquid Mercury**: Reflective, fluid, distortion.
7.  **Vantablack**: Absolute absorption, void-like.
8.  **Aerogel**: Blue smoke, ghost-like, solid air.
9.  **Carbon Fiber**: Woven texture, matte/gloss mix.
10. **Bioplastic**: Transparent organic, cellular details.

### B. Accent Materials (10% Details)
*Select 1 based on `(Minute) % 8`*
1.  **Neon Light**: Glowing tubes.
2.  **Gold Leaf**: Crinkled, luxury antique.
3.  **Moss/Grass**: Living organic texture.
4.  **Rusty Iron**: Orange oxidation, time-worn.
5.  **Holographic Foil**: Rainbow gradients.
6.  **Velvet**: Soft, light-absorbing cloth.
7.  **Crystal**: Faceted, prismatic refraction.
8.  **Porcelain**: Smooth, fragile white.

### C. Lighting Scenarios (The Mood)
*Select 1 based on `(Second) % 8`*
1.  **Softbox Studio**: Clean, shadowless, commercial (Default for Bento).
2.  **God Rays**: Volumetric light cutting through dust.
3.  **Bioluminescence**: Object glows from within (Dark bg).
4.  **Golden Hour**: Long shadows, warm orange sun.
5.  **Neon Noir**: Pink/Cyan rim lights, dark background.
6.  **Caustics**: Underwater light patterns, dappled.
7.  **Rembrandt**: High contrast, dramatic single source.
8.  **Flat Lay**: Even, top-down, no shadows (Graphic style).

### D. Composition Rules
1.  **Knolling**: 90-degree alignment (Order).
2.  **Floating**: Zero-gravity suspension (Dynamic).
3.  **Macro**: Extreme close-up on texture (Intimate).
4.  **Isometric**: 30-degree parallel projection (Technical).

## 2. Conflict Resolution
*   **Conflict**: Vantablack + Dark Background. -> **Fix**: Force Rim Lighting or Neon Accent.
*   **Conflict**: Concrete + Bioluminescence. -> **Fix**: Concrete must be cracked to let light through.
*   **Conflict**: Washi Paper + Liquid Mercury. -> **Fix**: Use Mercury as a container or spill *on* the paper.

## 3. Usage
When generating a prompt, if the user does NOT specify a style, **Roll the Dice** on these tables to generate a unique "Style DNA" for that specific request.
