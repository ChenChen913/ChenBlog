# Premium Food Infographic Poster

## Layout Structure
**Format**: 9:16 Vertical (Portrait)
**Grid**: Strict Magazine Grid (Left 1/3 Info Panel, Right 2/3 Hero Image)
**Style**: High-End Lifestyle Magazine Feature (Not a Menu, Not an Ad)

## Visual Hierarchy

### 1. Title & Hierarchy (Top Center)
- **Main Title**: "[DISH_NAME]" (Calm, editorial sans-serif).
- **Subtitle**: Cuisine origin / Regional reference (Small, understated, Bilingual).
- **Role**: Visual and emotional anchor.

### 2. Right 2/3 - Hero Dish Image (Full Screen)
- **Subject**: High-end commercial food photography.
- **Quality**: Refined plating, accurate textures (moisture, gloss, doneness).
- **Atmosphere**: Tableware and surface match cuisine/region.
- **Lighting**: Soft directional, natural shadows.

### 3. Left 1/3 - Ingredient Info Panel (Overlay)
- **Material**: Frosted / Translucent Glass (Museum annotation style).
- **Shape**: Rounded corners.
- **Styling**: Semi-transparent background, clean vertical rhythm, generous white space.
- **Graphics**: Fine white linework only, hand-drawn technical icons (ink/pencil style).
- **Text**: White text, no bright colors.

## Content Modules (Info Panel Focus: Trust & Origin)

### A. Primary Ingredients (核心食材)
- **Selection**: 4-6 specific, health-signaling items (e.g., "Grass-fed Free-range Ribeye").
- **Format**: Chinese first, English second.
- **Visuals**: Thin leader lines pointing to dish, small monochrome technical icons (leaf/grain/water).

### B. Secondary Ingredients (辅料/香料)
- **Selection**: Herbs, spices, oils, sauces.
- **Format**: Compact list or micro-grid.
- **Visuals**: Small outline icons (herb leaf, oil drop).

### C. Ingredient Provenance (源头可控)
- **Visuals**: 2-3 small photo-real source vignettes (Rectangles/Circles).
- **Subjects**: Natural growing/raising environments (e.g., Mountain pasture, Coastal waters).
- **Caption**: "Traceable Origin / 可追溯产地" (Bilingual).

## Design Language
- **Linework**: White only, slightly imperfect architectural sketch feel.
- **Tone**: Trust, not temptation. No flavor descriptors ("Delicious").
- **Texture**: Subtle film grain, matte contrast, editorial paper feel.
