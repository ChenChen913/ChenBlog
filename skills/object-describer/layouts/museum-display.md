# Museum Display Template (Minimalist)

## Layout Structure
**Format**: Square 1:1 or Portrait 4:5
**Composition**: Single Central Subject on Pedestal
**Style**: "White Cube" Gallery Aesthetic

## Elements

### 1. The Artifact (Center)
- **Subject**: [Object Name] treated as a rare artifact.
- **Micro-Innovation**: [Micro-Innovation Twist] (e.g., Fossilized, Floating, Deconstructed).
- **Lighting**: [Rembrandt] or [Spotlight] - dramatic focus.

### 2. The Pedestal
- **Material**: [Selected Base Material] (e.g., Marble, Concrete).
- **Form**: Simple geometric cylinder or cube.

### 3. The Plaque (Bottom Left or Right)
- **Material**: Gold/Silver/Glass plate.
- **Text**:
    - "Fig. 1: [Scientific Name]"
    - "Year: 2026"
    - "Origin: [Fictional Origin]"

### 4. Background
- **Style**: Infinite cyclorama or textured wall.
- **Color**: [Palette Background Color].

## Vibe
Quiet, Reverent, High-Value, timeless.
