# Material Specifications Library

## 1. Translucent Series (The "Air" Look)
*   **Frosted Acrylic**: `Backdrop-filter blur: 20px`, `Opacity: 80%`, `White-frost tint`.
*   **Aerogel**: `Blue smoke appearance`, `99% air`, `Ghostly solid`.
*   **Bioplastic**: `Cellular structure visible`, `Organic irregularities`, `Warm transparency`.
*   **Colored Glass**: `Stained glass effect`, `Sharp caustics`, `Gemstone clarity`.

## 2. Solid/Matte Series (The "Earth" Look)
*   **Raw Concrete**: `Porous texture`, `Air bubbles`, `Cold grey`, `Brutalist`.
*   **Sandstone**: `Grainy`, `Warm beige`, `Sedimentary layers`, `Ancient`.
*   **Matte Ceramic**: `Smooth but non-reflective`, `Eggshell finish`, `Soft touch`.
*   **Washi Paper**: `Fibrous`, `Visible pulp`, `Soft light diffusion`.

## 3. Reflective/Metallic Series (The "Water" Look)
*   **Liquid Mercury**: `Fluid simulation`, `High surface tension`, `Perfect mirror`.
*   **Brushed Aluminum**: `Anisotropic scratches`, `Satin finish`, `Tech feel`.
*   **Gold Leaf**: `Flaking texture`, `Rich yellow reflection`, `Antique luxury`.
*   **Iridescent Foil**: `Rainbow shift`, `Oil slick colors`, `Futuristic packaging`.

## 4. Exotic Series (The "Void" Look)
*   **Vantablack**: `Zero reflection`, `Silhouette only`, `Void texture`.
*   **Velvet**: `Light absorbing`, `Soft falloff`, `Rich depth`.
*   **Moss/Fungi**: `Fuzzy texture`, `Subsurface scattering`, `Living surface`.
