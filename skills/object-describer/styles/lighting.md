# Lighting & Atmosphere Library

## 1. Studio/Clean
*   **Softbox High-Key**: `White background`, `Shadowless`, `Even illumination`, `Product photography`.
*   **Knolling Light**: `Top-down flat light`, `Minimal shadows`, `Diagrammatic`.
*   **Ring Light**: `Circular highlight in reflections`, `Beauty lighting`.

## 2. Natural/Organic
*   **Golden Hour**: `Low angle sun`, `Warm orange cast`, `Long soft shadows`.
*   **Dappled Sunlight (Gobo)**: `Tree leaf shadows`, `Organic light patterns`, `Summer afternoon vibe`.
*   **Overcast/Diffused**: `Giant soft light`, `Low contrast`, `Moody grey`.

## 3. Dramatic/Cinematic
*   **Rembrandt**: `Single strong source`, `Deep shadows`, `High contrast`, `Emotional`.
*   **Volumetric (God Rays)**: `Hazy atmosphere`, `Visible light beams`, `Dust motes`, `Epic scale`.
*   **Rim Lighting**: `Backlight only`, `Glowing edges`, `Silhouette emphasis`.

## 4. Artificial/Futuristic
*   **Neon Noir**: `Dual color side lighting (Cyan/Magenta)`, `Dark background`, `Cyberpunk`.
*   **Bioluminescence**: `Light comes FROM the object`, `Dark surroundings`, `Glow effects`.
*   **Caustics**: `Underwater light refraction patterns`, `Wavy lines of light`.
